01 • Who We Are

Who We Are

Share a Smile Charity Club is a student-led initiative at Laikipia University dedicated to practical compassion, youth mentorship, environmental care, and peer solidarity.

Compassion in Action

We turn goodwill into organized outreach drives, supporting learners, families, and communities with dignity.

Student Mentorship

We guide and encourage students through peer mentorship, academic support, and positive decision-making.

Comrade Solidarity

We stand together as members during challenges and celebrations so every member feels supported.
