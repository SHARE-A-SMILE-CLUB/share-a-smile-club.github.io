Priority 6 • Mission & Core Pillars

Who We Are

One Smile, One Heart, One Community. We unite students at Laikipia University to serve vulnerable households, mentor learners, and support fellow comrades in need.

Mentorship & Guidance

Supporting students and younger learners with practical guidance, academic encouragement, and positive life choices.

Service & Sustainability

Partnering on environmental conservation, clean-up drives, and community resilience projects around Nyandarua and Laikipia.

Comrade Welfare

Standing together during emergencies, bereavement, or urgent needs so no member faces hardship alone.
