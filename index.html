2027 Calendar of Activities (January – December)

One Smile, One Heart, One Community.

Month Timing Activity Key Deliverable / Lead
January Mid-Month Membership Drive & Merch Distribution Reopen for Semester 2, verify member Reg. Numbers on the club website, and distribute club t-shirts/hoodies.
January Late Month Electoral Committee Setup & Nominations Appoint the 3-member Independent Electoral Committee and open nominations for all seats.
February Early Month Semester 2 Charity Outreach Visit Conduct Semester 2's official outreach visit, delivering food, clothing, and mentorship to a selected beneficiary.
February Mid-Month Vetting, Campaigns & Voter Register Vet candidates, run issue-based campaigns, and publish the verified voter register 48 hours before voting day.
February Late Month First Official Elections & Handover Conduct secret-ballot voting, announce verified results, and hand over from the interim executive to the newly elected committee.
March Mid-Month Campus Fundraising Drive Newly elected committee runs a student fundraiser (movie night, car wash, or merch push) to build the outreach kitty.
April Early Month Semester 2 Audit & Exam Break Treasurer, Club Auditor, and Storekeeper publish financial and donations reports before exams begin.
May Mid-Month Online Impact & Accountability Report Publish outreach photos, impact numbers, and financial summaries on the club website and socials.
June Mid-Month Sponsor & Well-Wisher Outreach Executive team pitches local businesses and well-wishers for cash or in-kind pledges.
July All Month "Smile at Home" Holiday Challenge Members carry out individual acts of kindness in their home counties and share updates on club channels.
August Late Month Virtual Executive Strategy Meeting Finalize the Semester 1 budget, freshers' recruitment plan, and outreach targets ahead of reopening.
September Mid-Month Freshers' Orientation & Recruitment Run a registration desk during orientation week and host a welcome meetup for new members.
October Mid-Month On-Campus Environmental & Awareness Day Partner with fellow university clubs for an on-campus tree-planting drive, clean-up, or awareness walk.
November Mid-Month Semester 1 Charity Outreach Visit Conduct Semester 1's official end-of-year donation drive and outreach visit to a selected beneficiary.
December Early Month Annual General Meeting (AGM) & Audit Present the full 2027 audited financial report, inventory logs, and 2028 plans before exams and holidays.

* Specific dates and outreach venues are confirmed by the Executive Committee ahead of each activity.

Who We Are

Our Mission

To mobilize students and well-wishers to uplift vulnerable communities through practical charity drives, mentorship, and solidarity.

Our Vision

A compassionate campus and community where every person feels supported, valued, and empowered to thrive.

Core Values

Empathy, transparency, teamwork, and consistent service—believing that even small daily contributions create lasting impact.

What We Do

Community Outreach

Organizing visits to children's homes, schools, and local families to share essential supplies, food, and encouragement.

Environmental Action

Partnering with campus clubs for tree-planting drives and clean-up campaigns to build a greener, healthier environment.

Student Solidarity

Standing together during tough times through member welfare drives, awareness campaigns, and peer mentorship.
