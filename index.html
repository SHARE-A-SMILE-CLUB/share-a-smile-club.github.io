01 • Who We Are

Who We Are

Share a Smile Charity Club is a student-led organization at Laikipia University committed to compassionate community service, youth mentorship, environmental care, and peer support.

Compassion in Action

We mobilize practical support, foodstuff pledges, and time to uplift vulnerable families and learners.

Student Mentorship

We foster peer encouragement, academic guidance, and leadership growth among university comrades.

Comrade Solidarity

We stand together as one body across all 146+ registered members through accountability and care.
